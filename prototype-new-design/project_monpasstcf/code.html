MonPassTCF

TCF preparation platform powered by AI to help students achieve their language certification goals.

Overview

MonPassTCF is a comprehensive ed-tech ecosystem designed to streamline the Test de Connaissance du Français (TCF) preparation process. Recognizing the stress and complexity of language certification, the platform provides a data-driven approach to learning, combining real-world simulations with advanced AI feedback loops.

The core mission was to build a tool that doesn't just present questions, but actively analyzes performance gaps and suggests targeted improvements, creating a personalized roadmap for every candidate.
