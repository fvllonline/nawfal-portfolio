MonPassTCF

The ultimate digital preparation companion for the Test de Connaissance du Français (TCF).

Overview

MonPassTCF is a comprehensive ed-tech platform designed to revolutionize how candidates prepare for the French language proficiency exam. Recognizing the high stakes and stress associated with international mobility, the platform offers a structured, data-driven approach to mastering the TCF exam components: listening, reading, structure, and writing.

The project was born from a need for more interactive and accessible preparation materials. Unlike traditional paper-based methods, MonPassTCF provides instant feedback through an intelligent scoring engine, allowing users to identify their linguistic weaknesses in real-time and focus their efforts where it matters most.

By leveraging modern web technologies, the platform provides a seamless, "app-like" experience across all devices, ensuring that students can practice during a commute or in a focused study session at home. The result is a premium, distraction-free environment that prioritizes learning efficiency and user confidence.

  • security

    Save progress across devices instantly, allowing for a continuous learning journey.

Challenges & Solutions

The Challenge: Real-time Audio Sync

Handling large audio assets while maintaining a responsive UI during timed tests was critical. Traditional loading led to latency that broke the exam immersion.

The Solution: Intelligent Pre-fetching

We implemented a service-worker based pre-fetching strategy that loads audio chunks in the background based on the user's predicted path through the exam, ensuring zero-latency playback.
